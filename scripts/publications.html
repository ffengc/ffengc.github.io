DriveReferee: Geometric Safety Verdicts Need Not Be Learned for Driving World-Action Models

arXiv preprint, 2026. Submitted to ICRA 2027.

Fengcheng Yu, Dhruv Parikh, Junjie Ye, Maulik Bhatt, Thang Vu, Igor Vasiljevic, Vitor Guizilini†, Yue Wang†

An analytic referee that executes the evaluator's known geometric safety rule on a scene state predicted from a single front camera, used both to distill safety preferences into a driving world-action model and to select safer candidates at deployment. Reaches 92.02 PDMS on the full NAVSIM navtest.

Deflickering Vision-Based Occupancy Networks through Lightweight Spatio-Temporal Correlation

Pattern Recognition (PR), 2026. Accepted.

Fengcheng Yu*, Haoran Xu*, Canming Xia, Ziyang Zong, Guang Tan†

OccLinker, a plug-in that mines motion from discarded high-frequency frames to correct current-frame predictions. Raises mIoU by up to +3.09 across 4 base occupancy networks at 2.29% added memory, and improves temporal consistency by up to +7.68.
